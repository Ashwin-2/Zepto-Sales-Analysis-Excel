# Zepto Sales Analysis 📊 | Excel Analytics & Interactive Dashboard

📌 Project Overview

This project is an end-to-end *Zepto Sales & Business Analytics project developed using Microsoft Excel*. The objective is to transform raw sales data into meaningful business insights and support data-driven decision-making.

🔄 Data Analysis Process

The project follows a complete data analytics workflow:

*Raw Data → Data Understanding → Data Cleaning → Data Transformation → Exploratory Data Analysis → KPI Analysis → PivotTables → PivotCharts → Interactive Dashboard → Business Insights*

> *Note:* This is a portfolio project created using a simulated dataset for learning and demonstration purposes. It does not represent Zepto's actual internal business data.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

The project focuses on answering key business questions:

- How is overall sales and revenue performing?
- Which product categories contribute the most to revenue?
- Which brands and products are top performers?
- Which customer segments generate the most revenue?
- How does customer behaviour vary across different demographics?
- Which cities contribute the highest sales?
- Which stores perform best?
- How efficiently are delivery partners performing?
- Which vehicle types handle the most deliveries?
- What is the current stock availability?
- What percentage of orders are out of stock?
- Where are the major opportunities for business improvement?

## 💡 Key Insights

- The dataset generated more than *₹3.60 Cr in sales* across *15,000 orders*.
- A total of *67,457 units* were sold with an average order value of approximately *₹2,398*.
- Revenue performance was analysed across different months and years to identify growth trends.
- *Snacks and Beverages* were among the leading revenue-generating categories.
- Customer analysis revealed differences in revenue contribution across *Premium, Regular and Occasional* customers.
- The average customer rating was approximately *3.45/5*.
- Brand-level analysis identified *HydroPlus and SnackHub* among the leading revenue contributors.
- Store-level analysis helped identify differences in performance across 50 stores.
- Delivery performance was analysed across *200 delivery partners* and multiple vehicle types.
- The *33.03% out-of-stock rate* indicates an opportunity to improve inventory planning and product availability.

## 💼 Business Recommendations

### 1. Improve Inventory Availability

Monitor high-demand products and improve inventory planning to reduce out-of-stock situations.

### 2. Focus on High-Performing Categories

Prioritise high-revenue categories such as Snacks and Beverages through promotions, cross-selling and better product availability.

### 3. Strengthen Customer Retention

Create targeted strategies for Premium, Regular and Occasional customers based on their purchasing behaviour.

### 4. Optimise Store Performance

Compare store-level KPIs to identify high-performing locations and understand the reasons behind underperformance.

### 5. Improve Delivery Operations

Analyse delivery partner and vehicle performance to improve delivery efficiency and resource allocation.

### 6. Monitor Customer Satisfaction

Track customer ratings along with sales and operational KPIs to identify areas affecting customer experience.
